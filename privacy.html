Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Introduction

LinkCash ("we", "us", "our") provides a URL shortening service that lets members earn money when their short links are visited. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. By using the website you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the service:

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored hashed) and payment details you provide to receive withdrawals.
  • Link data: the URLs you shorten, aliases and the statistics generated by visits to them.
  • Visitor data: IP address, approximate country, device and browser type, referrer and time on page, used to count valid views and detect fraud.
  • Messages: information you send us through the contact form or support channels.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account and process withdrawals.
  • To count views, calculate earnings and display statistics.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, spam and malicious links.
  • To send important notices such as payment, security and policy updates.
  • To improve the service and respond to support requests.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences and verify that a visit is a unique, valid view. Visitors must have JavaScript and cookies enabled for a view to be counted. You can disable cookies in your browser, but parts of the service may stop working.

5. Advertising Partners

Pages visitors see before reaching a destination link may display advertisements from third-party ad networks. These partners may use their own cookies and identifiers to show relevant ads and measure performance. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies, and we do not control it.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the website (hosting, email, payment processing, security scanning), when required by law, or to protect our rights, users and the public from fraud and abuse.

7. Data Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. Raw view logs are deleted after a limited period and kept only as daily summaries. We may retain certain records longer where required for accounting, legal or fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Security

We protect data with measures such as hashed passwords, encrypted connections, rate limiting and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children's Privacy

The service is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, email support@yourdomain.com or use the contact form.
