Views that are not paid

  • A repeat visit from the same visitor within 24 hours
  • Visitors who leave before the timer ends or skip a step
  • Bots, scripts, auto-refreshers and automated browsers
  • Traffic from VPN, proxy or datacenter networks
  • Views on links to adult, gambling, scam or malware content
  • Views flagged by our fraud checks (see below)

How earnings are released

Pending – new earnings wait for a short review period (set by the administrator) so invalid traffic can be filtered out.
Available – reviewed earnings you can withdraw once you reach the minimum payout.
Cancelled – earnings from invalid traffic or links that break our Terms of Use.

Fraud monitoring

We watch every account for unusual patterns, such as an abnormal ratio of views to clicks, many views from one IP address or IP range, or time-on-page that is too regular to be human. Accounts that trigger these alerts may be reviewed, new traffic sources may need approval, and earnings from suspicious traffic can be cancelled. Reports from advertising networks can also lead to adjustments. If you think a view was rejected by mistake, contact support with the short link and the date.
